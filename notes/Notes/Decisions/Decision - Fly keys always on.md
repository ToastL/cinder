**Decision:** WASD/QE fly the editor camera whenever the Scene view has focus, with no mouse button held — not Unity's right-button-only flying.

**Why:** the gizmo work made flying right-button-only so W/E/R could be tool keys. The user: "i dont like the idea what you can only move around with right button".

**How to apply:** editor shortcuts avoid W, A, S, D, Q and E while the Scene view has focus. Gizmo tools are 1/2/3, X toggles World/Local. See [[Editor camera]], [[Transform gizmos]].
